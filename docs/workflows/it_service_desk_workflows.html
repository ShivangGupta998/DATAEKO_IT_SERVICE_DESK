Onboarding Operating Principles

1. Admin/Manager RBAC Gate The Onboarding portal is guarded strictly by Manager/Admin roles. Any unauthenticated or regular Employee attempt triggers immediate 403 Forbidden deflection.
2. Zero-Touch Day 1 Readiness Accounts are created with randomized temporary passwords and auto-assigned to departmental distribution lists. Hardware is imaged with full disk encryption and MDM profiles.
3. Security & Self-Service Handoff On first login, users must reset their initial password, enroll in MFA, and are directed to the Knowledge Base self-service portal to accelerate ramp-up without manual IT intervention.

Key Operating Rules & SLA Milestones

90-Minute Queue Auto-Assign If both Manager and Admin are on leave or unresponsive for 90 minutes, tickets bypass manual triage and are automatically routed to the next available qualified technician.
Active Status Updates Technicians set priority and mark "In Progress". An automated email/Slack notification is immediately dispatched to the requester with tracking links.
Rejection & Deflection If reviewers reject the ticket (e.g. out of scope, unauthorized), the ticket is closed with mandatory reviewer comments and the employee is notified with alternative self-service paths.
